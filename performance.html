A large prefill changes the latency story

A 128K cold prefill collided with four short streams in three paired, randomized repetitions.

Arrival orderSmall TTFT p95Small aggregate decodeLong TTFTLong prefill
Large prefill first+1,035.76%-44.05%+15.36%-13.31%
Small streams already active-0.68%-44.97%+17.55%-14.93%

The scheduling lesson is concrete: a large prefill arriving first blocks short requests' first token. If short streams already started, their TTFT survives, but their ongoing decode is still almost halved.
